Research interests

  • Reinforcement learning for language models — preference-based post-training (RLHF, DPO) and RL with verifiable rewards for reasoning (GRPO, R1-style training).
  • Vision-language and vision-language-action models — connecting visual encoders to LLMs (LLaVA, Qwen2-VL), making them reason with images (DeepEyes), and turning them into robot policies (π0, π0.7).
